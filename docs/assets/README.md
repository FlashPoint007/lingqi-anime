## Generation briefs / 生成设计说明

**Logo:** A simple flat 07 monogram for Lingqi Anime, with an open zero/crescent joined to an angular seven and a play cutout. Dark jade `#21665a`, warm ivory `#fffefa`, gold `#f1ce52`. Japanese editorial identity; solid clean shapes, clear margin, no words, mascot, known IP, third-party branding, 3D or mockup.

**Cover:** A horizontal 3:1 Japanese editorial cover using the approved logo; exact text “零柒番剧”, “LINGQI ANIME”, “发现好故事，接着看下去”. Clean title area on the left; original anime-background-style dusk study window, skyline, lamplight and subtle film details on the right. Jade, cream and gold; quiet evening mood; no character, existing IP, URL, fake website interface or watermark.
